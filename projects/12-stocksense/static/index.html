      How StockSense decides

      Every step is plain Python you can read

      1. Clean stock-out days (flagged, or detected when a zero is implausible: Poisson P(0) < 1%) are imputed so censored sales don't drag forecasts down.
      2. De-festival last year's festival spikes are measured per SKU and divided out.
      3. Forecast with 4 models: seasonal naive, moving average, Holt-Winters (additive, weekly), regression (day-of-week + festival + promo).
      4. Backtest 4 rolling-origin folds × 14 days; the lowest sMAPE wins per SKU.
      5. Re-apply festival uplift for upcoming festivals (e.g. Diwali).
      6. Optimise safety stock z·σ, reorder point, EOQ, shelf-life cap, case-pack rounding.
      7. Explain every order with its numbers, risk and rupee impact.
