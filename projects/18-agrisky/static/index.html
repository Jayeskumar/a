k-means zoning

Bare soil (NDVI < 0.35) and open water (NDWI > 0) are masked first; crop cells are z-scored on NDVI, NDRE, NDWI, SAVI, clustered with k-means++, smoothed with a 3×3 mode filter and split into connected zones.
