### StudyPilot — Your offline AI study companion

*Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning* · [code](../projects/01-studypilot/) · [report](../projects/01-studypilot/REPORT.md) · [deck](../decks/01-studypilot.pptx) · `cd projects/01-studypilot && python app.py` → http://localhost:8001

![StudyPilot](../projects/01-studypilot/docs/screenshot.png)

**Problem**

- Students drown in pages of notes before exams
- Re-reading is slow; without review, material fades in days
- AI chatbots need internet and paid keys, and can hallucinate
- Tier-2/3 colleges often have weak Wi-Fi and costly mobile data

**What it does**

- **TextRank summary** — Adjustable length, per-sentence scores and a similarity graph
- **Key concepts** — RAKE keyphrases as weighted chips with highlighted evidence
- **Auto quiz** — Cloze and MCQ with similar-type distractors and typo-tolerant grading
- **SM-2 flashcards** — Auto-made cards, Again/Hard/Good/Easy, schedule saved locally
- **Ask your notes** — BM25 answers with highlights, confidence and term breakdown
- **Exam readiness** — Mastery per concept from quiz and card history, plus study-next

**Algorithms (all from scratch)**

- TextRank (PageRank power iteration) – extractive summary
- RAKE + occurrence boost – key-concept extraction
- Type-similarity ranking – MCQ distractor selection
- Okapi BM25 + definition bonus – question answering
- SM-2 – spaced-repetition scheduling
- Laplace-smoothed mastery – exam-readiness score

**Measured results**

- **0.526** — ROUGE-1 F1 vs reference (Lead-6: 0.465)
- **100%** — QA top-3 hit rate on 31 questions (top-1 80.6%)
- **~6 ms** — full analysis of a 780-word note set
- **~1 ms** — Ask-your-notes HTTP round trip

### WattWise — AI energy & carbon copilot for smart campuses

*Sustainability, CleanTech & Green Energy* · [code](../projects/02-wattwise/) · [report](../projects/02-wattwise/REPORT.md) · [deck](../decks/02-wattwise.pptx) · `cd projects/02-wattwise && python app.py` → http://localhost:8002

![WattWise](../projects/02-wattwise/docs/screenshot.png)

**Problem**

- Labs, ACs and lights run all night and on weekends
- The only feedback is a monthly bill, weeks late
- No one knows if rooftop solar would really pay off
- Green drives fade because savings are never measured

**What it does**

- **7-day forecast** — Hourly per building, split into calendar baseline + weather effect
- **Waste detective** — Robust z-score alerts: '18 kW at 3 AM Sun vs 3 kW typical'
- **Carbon ledger** — kWh to rupees to kg CO2 to trees, with named, editable factors
- **Solar planner** — kWp, payback, 25-year cash flow and CO2 avoided for any roof
- **What-if levers** — AC setpoint, auto-off, LED and BLDC - live savings, no double count
- **Green leaderboard** — Weather-normalised savings vs own baseline; Green Champion badge

**Algorithms (all from scratch)**

- Weighted ridge regression (Gaussian elimination) - hourly load forecast
- Robust trimming from category medians - waste never becomes 'normal'
- Modified z-score (median + MAD) - waste event detection
- Holt-Winters triple smoothing - benchmark forecaster
- Degraded cash-flow model - solar payback and 25-year savings
- Weather-normalised baseline (IPMVP-style) - fair leaderboard

**Measured results**

- **5.6%** — forecast MAPE on hold-out week (Holt-Winters 27.7%)
- **100% / 89%** — waste-event precision / recall vs injected truth
- **10%** — monthly saving from 4 cheap levers (Rs 10.7 L/yr, demo)
- **300 ms** — full pipeline for 6 buildings x 8 weeks

### PulseGuard — Early warning for patient deterioration, before it's too late

*HealthTech & Biomedical Innovation* · [code](../projects/03-pulseguard/) · [report](../projects/03-pulseguard/REPORT.md) · [deck](../decks/03-pulseguard.pptx) · `cd projects/03-pulseguard && python app.py` → http://localhost:8003

![PulseGuard](../projects/03-pulseguard/docs/screenshot.png)

**Problem**

- Ward patients deteriorate silently between manual observation rounds
- Understaffed district hospitals: one nurse, many beds, paper charts
- Paper NEWS2 is a snapshot; nobody plots the trend
- Threshold alarms fire only after the patient has crossed the line

**What it does**

- **NEWS2 engine** — All 7 parameters, Scale 1/2, red-3 rule, 4 bands, RCP responses
- **Trend early warning** — OLS slopes + R² gating flag rising risk before thresholds
- **Live ward simulator** — 12 seeded patients, server clock, play/pause/speed/reset
- **Explainability** — Per-parameter points, zone-shaded charts, rule that fired
- **Bedside calculator** — Validated entry, instant score, save to patient chart
- **Auto handover** — Printable per-patient summary: score, trend, last change

**Algorithms (all from scratch)**

- NEWS2 (RCP 2017) – aggregate risk score and clinical response
- Ordinary least squares – per-vital slope and R² over 3 h
- Trend projection – score projected vitals with NEWS2
- Hysteresis state machine – alert without flicker spam
- AR(1) physiological simulator – seeded deterministic vitals

**Measured results**

- **1.0 h** — median extra warning vs NEWS2≥5 alert (held-out sim)
- **3.25 h** — median earlier than 4-hourly paper rounds (held-out sim)
- **3 / 270** — false trend alerts per stable patient-days (held-out)
- **≈6 µs** — NEWS2 scoring latency per reading

### CertChain — Tamper-proof academic credentials, verified in seconds

*Cybersecurity & Blockchain Technology* · [code](../projects/04-certchain/) · [report](../projects/04-certchain/REPORT.md) · [deck](../decks/04-certchain.pptx) · `cd projects/04-certchain && python app.py` → http://localhost:8004

![CertChain](../projects/04-certchain/docs/screenshot.png)

**Problem**

- Fake degree, marksheet and internship certificates keep fooling recruiters
- A doctored PDF takes minutes; manual college verification takes days to weeks
- Phone/email checks leave no audit trail and can be socially engineered
- Central databases must be trusted blindly - insider edits are invisible

**What it does**

- **Issue & bulk CSV** — Form or CSV batch -> one mined, signed block per batch
- **Printable cert + QR** — QR code from our own pure-Python encoder, ID and 8-char verify code
- **Explainable verify** — Hash, Merkle proof, block hash, PoW, signature, anchoring, revocation
- **Revocation registry** — Issuer-signed revocations recorded on-chain; only the issuer can revoke
- **Tamper demo** — Edit a CGPA in chain.json and watch the exact block and link break
- **Block explorer** — Linked block cards with hashes, nonces, signatures and check status

**Algorithms (all from scratch)**

- Ed25519 (RFC 8032) – issuer signatures on blocks and revocations
- SHA-256 canonical-JSON hashing with 128-bit salt – record fingerprint
- Merkle tree + inclusion proofs – verify one cert against a header
- Proof-of-work (4 hex zeros) – cost to rewrite history
- Full-chain validation – links, PoW, roots, signatures, rules
- QR encoding with Reed-Solomon GF(256) – scannable verify link

**Measured results**

- **33 ms** — end-to-end certificate verification (median)
- **3.4 / 3.8 ms** — Ed25519 sign / verify, pure Python
- **4 / 4** — RFC 8032 test vectors matched
- **8 / 8** — tamper scenarios detected in tests

![KrishiMitra](../projects/05-krishimitra/docs/screenshot.png)

**Problem**

- Crops chosen by habit, not by soil and season
- Urea over-used, P and K neglected; money and soil wasted
- Irrigation by guesswork; produce sold at the harvest-glut low
- Advisory apps are English-first, online-only black boxes

**What it does**

- **Fertiliser advisor** — NPK deficit to Urea / DAP / MOP kg per acre, bags, organic options
- **Irrigation planner** — FAO-56 Ra + Hargreaves ET0 x Kc: 7-day plan in mm and litres/acre
- **Market timing** — Seasonal index + trend: sell now or hold N weeks, with price band

- Gaussian Naive Bayes – crop probabilities + log-odds per input
- CART + Random Forest – bootstrap, feature bagging, OOB, Saabas
- FAO-56 Ra + Hargreaves ET0 – reference water demand
- Kc stage curve + soil bucket – 7-day irrigation schedule
- Ratio-to-moving-average seasonal index + OLS trend – sell/hold

**Measured results**

- **94.3%** — hold-out accuracy, GNB+RF blend (synthetic, 14 crops)
- **93.6%** — GNB and RF each, on the same hold-out
- **2.9 ms** — median HTTP latency, full advisory
- **32.2** — Ra MJ/m²/day: matches FAO-56 Example 8

### PlaceIQ — Placement analytics that tells every student what to fix next

*Data Science & Analytics* · [code](../projects/06-placeiq/) · [report](../projects/06-placeiq/REPORT.md) · [deck](../decks/06-placeiq.pptx) · `cd projects/06-placeiq && python app.py` → http://localhost:8006

![PlaceIQ](../projects/06-placeiq/docs/screenshot.png)

**Problem**

- Placement cells track hundreds of students in spreadsheets
- They learn who got placed only after the drive is over
- Students don't know which skill gap matters most
- Departments can't see weak trends early enough to act

**What it does**

- **What to fix next** — Exact minimum-effort counterfactual plan per student, ranked by effort
- **Why this prediction** — Coefficient x standardised value bars that add up to the score
- **What-if simulator** — 12 sliders with a live probability gauge and re-planning
- **At-risk list** — Threshold slider, top gap per student, one-click CSV export
- **Student personas** — k-means++ clusters such as Coding stars and Toppers lacking projects
- **Fairness check** — Per-department FPR, FNR and calibration gap on out-of-fold predictions

**Algorithms (all from scratch)**

- Logistic regression (gradient descent, L2) - placement probability
- Ridge linear regression (normal equations) - expected package
- Bounded knapsack DP - minimum-effort counterfactual plan
- k-means++ - student personas
- Rank-based ROC-AUC, Pearson correlation - evaluation and EDA

**Measured results**

- **0.935** — ROC-AUC on 150 held-out students
- **86.0%** — hold-out accuracy (baseline 58.0%)
- **0.588** — package R2, MAE 1.17 LPA
- **2.7 s** — cold start: data + 9 models, pure Python

### PhishGuard — Explainable scam & phishing detector for SMS, email and links

*Cybersecurity & Blockchain Technology* · [code](../projects/07-phishguard/) · [report](../projects/07-phishguard/REPORT.md) · [deck](../decks/07-phishguard.pptx) · `cd projects/07-phishguard && python app.py` → http://localhost:8007

![PhishGuard](../projects/07-phishguard/docs/screenshot.png)

**Problem**

- Daily flood of fake KYC, EB-bill, parcel, lottery and UPI-collect SMS
- Students targeted by fake internships that demand a 'registration fee'
- Spam filters are black boxes: users never learn why a message is dangerous
- Link checkers need the internet and upload your private messages

**What it does**

- **Explanation panel** — Rules fired, NB token weights, link feature contributions, highlighted spans
- **Link analyser** — 28 lexical features: IP host, @ trick, punycode, look-alike brands, cheap TLDs
- **India-first rules** — KYC/PAN, EB cut, UPI collect, job fee, OTP, digital arrest; Tanglish/Hinglish
- **Bulk scan** — Paste 200 lines, get a table of verdicts and main reasons in milliseconds
- **Spot the scam quiz** — 10-question awareness game with explanations and the model's verdict
- **What to do** — Clear next steps: call 1930, report on cybercrime.gov.in, block sender

**Algorithms (all from scratch)**

- Multinomial Naive Bayes (words, bigrams, char 3-4-grams, Laplace) - text
- Logistic regression (gradient descent + L2) on 28 URL features - links
- Homoglyph map + edit distance - look-alike brand domains
- Negation-aware regex rules - India-specific scam patterns
- Noisy-OR evidence fusion with temperature + prior calibration

**Measured results**

- **97%** — accuracy on unseen message templates (n=168)
- **94%** — URL model accuracy, 100% precision (n=110)
- **~1 ms** — full analysis per message (p50, pure Python)
- **~1 s** — training both models from scratch
